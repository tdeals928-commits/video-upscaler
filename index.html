Save opens the Share sheet (choose Save Video → Photos, or send to an app). Download .zip saves one file with every clip (goes to Files / Downloads). On big batches, saving one at a time is most reliable.
Engine loads on first run (~30 MB, cached after).

⚡ Runs 100% on your phone with ffmpeg (WebAssembly). Videos are processed one at a time. Best for clips under ~1 minute — long clips or very large batches may run out of memory (save finished ones, then reload).
